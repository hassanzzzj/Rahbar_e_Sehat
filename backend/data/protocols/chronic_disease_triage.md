# Chronic Disease Management Triage

## Diabetes Mellitus

### Emergency (EMERGENCY)
- Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) signs:
  - Blood glucose >250 mg/dL
  - Kussmaul breathing (deep, rapid)
  - Fruity breath odor
  - Altered consciousness
  - Severe dehydration
- Hypoglycemia with altered consciousness
  - Blood glucose <54 mg/dL
  - Unable to take oral glucose

**Immediate Actions:**
- Check blood glucose immediately
- IV access
- If hypoglycemic: IV dextrose or IM glucagon
- If hyperglycemic: IV insulin protocol

**Referral:** DHQ with ICU

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Persistent hyperglycemia (>300 mg/dL) despite medication
- Recurrent hypoglycemia episodes
- Foot ulcer or infection
- Visual changes (suspect retinopathy)

**Referral:** THQ diabetic clinic

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- HbA1c monitoring
- Medication adjustment
- Diet and lifestyle counseling
- Annual screening (eyes, feet, kidneys)

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

## Hypertension

### Emergency (EMERGENCY)
- Hypertensive crisis: BP >180/120 mmHg WITH:
  - Chest pain (suspect MI or aortic dissection)
  - Neurological deficits (suspect stroke)
  - Acute kidney injury
  - Pulmonary edema
  - Papilledema

**Referral:** DHQ emergency department

### Urgent (URGENT)
- BP >180/120 mmHg without acute organ damage
- BP >160/100 mmHg with symptoms (headache, dizziness)
- Hypertension in pregnancy

**Referral:** THQ medical clinic

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- BP 140-179/90-119 mmHg
- Medication adjustment
- Lifestyle modification counseling

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mildly elevated BP 130-139/85-89 mmHg
- No other risk factors

**Advice:** DASH diet, salt restriction, exercise, weight management, regular monitoring

## Asthma/COPD

### Emergency (EMERGENCY)
- Severe exacerbation (see emergency protocols)
- Peak flow <50% personal best
- Oxygen saturation <92%

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Increased use of rescue inhaler (>3 times/week)
- Night symptoms >1/week
- Peak flow 50-75% personal best
- Recent hospitalization for exacerbation

**Referral:** THQ respiratory clinic

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Stable on current medication
- Annual review
- Inhaler technique check

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mild intermittent symptoms
- Peak flow >80% personal best
- No night symptoms

**Advice:** Adherence to controller medication, trigger avoidance, action plan education

## Important Notes
- Chronic disease patients often present late — maintain low threshold for urgent referral
- Check medication adherence before escalating treatment
- Consider cost barriers to care in Pakistan context
- Provide written action plans in Urdu/local language
