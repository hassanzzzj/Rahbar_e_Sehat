# Pediatric Triage Guidelines (Ages 0-12)

## Fever in Children

### Urgent Referral (URGENT)
- Age <3 months with temperature >38°C
- Temperature >40°C not responding to antipyretics
- Fever lasting >5 days
- Non-blanching rash (petechiae, purpura)
- Lethargy, poor feeding, irritability
- Signs of dehydration (sunken fontanelle, dry mucous membranes)
- Seizure (first episode or prolonged >5 min)

**Referral:** THQ or DHQ pediatric unit

### Routine Care (ROUTINE)
- Fever 38-39.5°C responding to paracetamol
- Child alert, feeding reasonably well
- No danger signs
- Duration <3 days

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mild fever <38°C
- Child active and playful
- Normal feeding and hydration
- Duration <24 hours

**Advice:** Paracetamol 15mg/kg every 4-6 hours, oral rehydration, monitor temperature

## Respiratory Distress

### Emergency (EMERGENCY)
- Severe lower chest wall indrawing
- Stridor at rest
- Oxygen saturation <90%
- Central cyanosis
- Unable to drink or feed
- Apnea episodes

**Referral:** Emergency department, THQ or above

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Fast breathing (age-adjusted):
  - <2 months: >60/min
  - 2-12 months: >50/min
  - 1-5 years: >40/min
- Moderate chest indrawing
- Wheezing not responding to bronchodilators
- Oxygen saturation 90-94%

**Referral:** RHC or THQ

## Diarrhea and Vomiting

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Signs of severe dehydration (lethargic, sunken eyes, very slow skin pinch)
- Bloody diarrhea
- Persistent vomiting (unable to keep fluids down)
- No urine output >8 hours
- Age <6 months

**Referral:** THQ or DHQ for IV rehydration

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Some dehydration (restless, sunken eyes, slow skin pinch)
- Able to drink ORS
- No blood in stool
- Duration <3 days

**Referral:** BHU or RHC, ORS and zinc supplementation

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mild diarrhea, no dehydration signs
- Child drinking well
- Alert and active

**Advice:** ORS after each loose stool, zinc 20mg/day for 10 days (>6 months), continue feeding
