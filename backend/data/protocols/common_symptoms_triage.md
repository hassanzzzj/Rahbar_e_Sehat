# Common Symptom Triage Protocols

## Fever in Adults

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Temperature >40°C
- Fever >5 days duration
- Associated with:
  - Severe headache, neck stiffness (meningitis)
  - Altered mental status
  - Non-blanching rash
  - Severe abdominal pain
  - Signs of sepsis (tachycardia, hypotension, tachypnea)
- Recent travel to malaria-endemic area
- Immunocompromised patient

**Referral:** THQ or DHQ

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Fever 38-40°C for 2-5 days
- Mild to moderate symptoms
- No danger signs
- Able to maintain oral intake

**Referral:** BHU or RHC for evaluation and basic investigations

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Low-grade fever <38.5°C
- Duration <48 hours
- Mild symptoms (headache, body aches)
- No risk factors

**Advice:** Paracetamol 500mg-1g every 6 hours, rest, oral fluids, monitor temperature

## Respiratory Symptoms

### Emergency (EMERGENCY)
- Severe breathlessness at rest
- Oxygen saturation <92%
- Chest pain with respiratory symptoms
- Hemoptysis (coughing blood) >1 tablespoon
- Altered consciousness

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Productive cough >2 weeks (suspect TB)
- Wheezing not responding to inhalers
- Pleuritic chest pain
- Oxygen saturation 92-94%

**Referral:** THQ for chest X-ray and sputum examination

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Cough 1-2 weeks
- Mild wheezing responding to bronchodilators
- Upper respiratory tract infection symptoms

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Cough <7 days
- Mild cold/flu symptoms
- No fever or low-grade fever
- No breathlessness

**Advice:** Steam inhalation, honey for cough (>1 year age), rest, fluids

## Gastrointestinal Symptoms

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Severe abdominal pain (any cause)
- Hematemesis (vomiting blood)
- Melena (black tarry stools) or hematochezia (fresh blood in stool)
- Persistent vomiting >24 hours
- Signs of intestinal obstruction (colicky pain, distension, absolute constipation)

**Referral:** THQ or DHQ surgical unit

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Abdominal pain 1-3 days, mild to moderate
- Diarrhea 2-5 days without dehydration
- Constipation >1 week
- Dyspepsia/reflux symptoms

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mild diarrhea <48 hours
- Occasional heartburn
- Mild constipation
- Mild food intolerance symptoms

**Advice:** ORS, bland diet, avoid spicy/fatty foods, monitor symptoms

## Pain Management

### Urgent (URGENT)
- Severe pain not controlled by paracetamol/ibuprofen
- Chest pain (cardiac workup needed)
- Severe headache (thunderclap, first/worst)
- Pain with neurological deficits

**Referral:** THQ or DHQ

### Routine (ROUTINE)
- Moderate pain affecting daily activities
- Chronic pain requiring investigation
- Joint pain with swelling

**Referral:** BHU or RHC

### Self-Care (SELF_CARE)
- Mild pain
- Headache responding to analgesics
- Minor musculoskeletal pain

**Advice:** Paracetamol 500mg-1g every 6 hours (max 4g/day), rest, ice/heat as appropriate
